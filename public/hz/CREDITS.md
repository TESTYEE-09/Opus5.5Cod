# Horizon Japan credits

Car models (from Sketchfab, via an open asset repository), optimised for the web:
- Corvette C7, Porsche 911 (992), Lamborghini Urus: see the original Sketchfab pages
- Ferrari 458, LaFerrari Aperta, Lamborghini Huracán GT3: VTX (CC BY-NC-SA)
- Jeep Wrangler Rubicon: vecarz (CC BY-NC-SA)
- McLaren MP4/5, Toyota GR Supra GT300: vecarz (CC BY)

Procedurally modelled in this project: Corvette C8 Z06 / ZR1 / ZR1X / E-Ray, C6 ZR1, C5 Z06,
Honda NSX / NSX-R, Dodge Viper GTS, all traffic, buildings, landmarks and trees.

Engine sound banks: Stunt Rally 3 engine-sim recordings (CC BY 4.0).
Textures: Poly Haven (CC0) and three.js examples.
Radio music: synthesised live in the browser.
